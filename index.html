Greeny

IoT garden management · UX/UI design

2 months

Greeny brings garden sensor readings and connected-device controls into one mobile interface. The dashboard combines soil-moisture data, weather information and garden overviews, connecting physical devices with the information caretakers need to manage their gardens.

Moti-Pulse

Motivation & goals · UX research and UI design

1 month

Moti-Pulse explores how personal purpose can connect with everyday action. The app brings life missions, goals and tasks together, with a calendar for planning. The project followed a research-led UX/UI process, from understanding motivation to developing the interface.

Synergy

University app · UX/UI case study

3 months

Synergy is a university app concept that brings campus tools and communication together. Its Online Meetings screen presents recommended sessions, registered and created meetings, and upcoming meeting cards, helping students find sessions and manage their participation in one place.
